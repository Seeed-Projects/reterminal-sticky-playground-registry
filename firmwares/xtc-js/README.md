# XTC.js

For Sticky with CrossPoint Reader: convert comics and PDFs to XTC in your browser.

- Author: varo6 and sodaFMR
- Source: https://github.com/varo6/xtcjs
- License: MIT
- Destination: https://xtcjs.app/
- Destination domain: xtcjs.app
- Status: draft, awaiting maintainer review

## Why a usable .bin cannot be supplied

The product is a browser application that converts reading files rather than firmware installed on the ESP32-S3. It produces XTC documents, so there is no device firmware .bin to submit. The maintained converter runs on xtcjs.app.

## Complete Sticky workflow and prerequisites

Install CrossPoint Reader on Sticky, open xtcjs.app, select Sticky as the target device, choose a local CBZ/CBR or PDF, adjust and preview the conversion, download the XTC file, transfer it to the reader, and read the converted pages on Sticky.
